Privacy Policy for Velmora

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Velmora ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Velmora mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and account credentials
  • Health & Fertility Data: Menstrual cycle dates, ovulation data, symptoms, pregnancy information
  • Baby Development Data: Child's date of birth, growth measurements, milestones, feeding and sleep logs
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app usage data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide personalized fertility tracking and cycle predictions
  • Improve our app features and user experience
  • Provide customer support

3. Children's Privacy

Velmora allows parents to track their baby's development from birth to age 2. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. All children's data is entered and managed by the parent or guardian. Parents can request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting us.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal and health data. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • With trusted service providers who assist in operating our app (bound by confidentiality agreements)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Export your data

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the date above.
